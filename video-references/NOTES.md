## Common patterns
- All are vertical (9:16 or close) and very short, so each is a logo or brand sting rather than a narrated video.
- The look is dark and glossy with glowing accents. Our `first-100` video is bright and colourful, so this is a different direction.
- Motion is fast, with an obvious beat and a clean final hold on the brand name.
- 02 and 03 look like AI-generated or heavily composited footage, not plain motion graphics.

## Patterns from references 04-06 (longer, with voiceover)
- **Word-highlight captions:** one word is boxed or lit while the rest is dimmed, synced to the voice. Our `first-100` video used a similar karaoke pill; the Zenith video has no captions by request.
- **A visual for every phrase:** each spoken phrase gets its own simple illustration or UI card, so nothing stays on screen for more than about 2 seconds.
- **Numbers as the hook:** count-ups (7,000,000,000+, balance counters) hold attention.
- **Comment-to-get call to action:** 04 and 05 end on a fake comment thread that shows the exact word to comment and a DM reply.
- **Background switches:** 05 changes from black to light grey halfway to reset attention. 06 stays in one strict black-and-white style.
- **Audio was not analysed:** these files have sound, but I only looked at frames.
