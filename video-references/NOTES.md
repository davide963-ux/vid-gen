## Common patterns
- All are vertical (9:16 or close) and very short, so each is a logo or brand sting rather than a narrated video.
- The look is dark and glossy with glowing accents. Our `first-100` video is bright and colourful, so this is a different direction.
- Motion is fast, with an obvious beat and a clean final hold on the brand name.
- 02 and 03 look like AI-generated or heavily composited footage, not plain motion graphics.
