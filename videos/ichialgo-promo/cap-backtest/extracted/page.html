What this does not prove

  • One period. Dec 3, 2025 – Sep 23, 2026 is about nine months. A rule can fit nine months of one regime and fail the next.
  • The pairs are correlated. EUR/USD, GBP/USD and AUD/USD are largely the same dollar trade, so 1017 trades are worth fewer than 1017 independent observations — the interval above is narrower than it should be.
  • It is all in-sample. Every setting on this page was chosen after seeing these candles. Trying filters until one looks good is how noise gets promoted to a strategy.
  • Costs are a flat estimate. Real spreads widen at the open, on news and overnight, exactly when the EMA tends to get touched. Swap on positions held overnight is not modelled at all.
