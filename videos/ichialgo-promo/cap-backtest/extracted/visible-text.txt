[h2] What this does not prove
[strong] One period.
[li] Dec 3, 2025 – Sep 23, 2026
[li] is about nine months. A rule can fit nine months of one regime and fail the next.
[strong] The pairs are correlated.
[li] EUR/USD, GBP/USD and AUD/USD are largely the same dollar trade, so
[li] 1017
[li] trades are worth fewer than
[li] 1017
[li] independent observations — the interval above is narrower than it should be.
[strong] It is all in-sample.
[li] Every setting on this page was chosen after seeing these candles. Trying filters until one looks good is how noise gets promoted to a strategy.
[strong] Costs are a flat estimate.
[li] Real spreads widen at the open, on news and overnight, exactly when the EMA tends to get touched. Swap on positions held overnight is not modelled at all.
