[h2] The story of
[h2] Amara Hotel & SPA
[p] Nestled along the breathtaking Albanian coastline, Amara Hotel is more than just a place to stay—it’s an experience designed to create unforgettable memories. With a commitment to excellence, we bring together the warmth of hospitality, modern elegance, and the tranquility of the Adriatic Sea to offer you a stay like no other. Our journey began with a simple dream: to create a haven of relaxation and luxury where every guest feels at home. Inspired by the beauty of our surroundings and the vibrant energy of Albania, Amara Hotel opened its doors to guests seeking a perfect blend of comfort, culture, and serenity.
[em] A Legacy of Hospitality
[p] At Amara Hotel, every detail is carefully curated to ensure a seamless and enjoyable experience. From our thoughtfully designed rooms with stunning sea views to our exquisite dining options featuring local and international cuisine, we strive to create a space where you can unwind, recharge, and reconnect. Our team of passionate professionals is dedicated to making your stay exceptional. Whether it’s a warm greeting at check-in, personalized recommendations for exploring the area, or a perfectly crafted cocktail by the pool, we are here to ensure every moment feels special.
[em] A Place to Create Memories
[p] Amara Hotel is more than a destination; it’s a story waiting to be written. Celebrate life’s special moments with us—whether it’s a romantic getaway, a family vacation, or a corporate retreat. Relax in our luxurious spa, enjoy a romantic dinner by the sea, or simply soak in the peaceful ambiance of our surroundings.

[span] our mission
[span] our vision
[p] Our mission is to provide our guests with exceptional hospitality that combines modern elegance, personalized care, and an authentic Albanian experience. We are committed to creating unforgettable moments through world-class accommodations, delightful culinary experiences, and a serene atmosphere that invites relaxation and joy. At Amara Hotel, we embrace innovation and sustainability, ensuring every aspect of our services meets the highest standards while preserving the environment and honoring the cultural legacy of Albania. Through our dedication, we aim to build meaningful connections with every guest, leaving them inspired and eager to return.
[p] At Amara Hotel, we aspire to redefine the standards of hospitality in Albania and the wider region by becoming a symbol of luxury, comfort, and cultural connection. Our vision is to create a haven where guests can immerse themselves in the tranquil beauty of the Adriatic, experience the rich heritage of Albania, and enjoy unparalleled service that fosters lasting memories. We strive to be a leader in sustainable tourism, harmonizing our operations with the natural environment while contributing to the local community’s growth and well-being.
